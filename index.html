About

Hi, I'm Alex! I'm a senior at Emory University completing my honors thesis with Dr. Li Xiong on membership inference attacks against sequence models, funded by the NSF CISE REU. I also work with Dr. Laxman Dhulipala at the University of Maryland on parallel disk algorithms for multi-SSD machines. I still maintain the library repository (https://github.com/Cyan9644/plaidlaylib) and plan to submit a paper on it soon. My current research interests are in the theory and practice of parallel algorithms, operating systems, and data privacy. I especially enjoy studying cache-oblivious and cache-aware algorithms at the moment. Gaps in latency between computing methods are my favorite topic to explore in research, both from disk to DRAM and DRAM to cache.
